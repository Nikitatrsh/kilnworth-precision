## Images

No photographs are used. The part drawings, shop floor plan, tolerance ruler, 404 drawing, logo and favicon are original SVG made in code for this project (`src/components/drawings/`, `src/components/Logo.astro`, `public/favicon.svg`). `public/og.png` is a screenshot of the site's own `/og-card` page.

The brief allowed free photos from Unsplash or Pexels with a uniform treatment; the drawings carried the design without them, and stock photos of machine shops tend to show recognizable machine brands, so none were added.
