## The job

A packaging machine builder (fictional for this demo) was switching a manifold from anodized aluminum to 316L for washdown environments. Fourteen ports intersect inside the block, and any burr left at an intersection eventually breaks off and jams a valve.

## What we did

- Reviewed the drawing before quoting and suggested moving two port centers by 0.040 in, so the intersections met at full diameter instead of a sliver.
- Milled on a horizontal setup with a 4th-axis rotary, all ports from one clamping.
- Deburred intersections with back-chamfer tools, then checked every part with a borescope.

## Result

60 manifolds in 9 working days, all passing a 100 psi leak test at the customer. The port change made it into the drawing at revision C.
