## The job

A defense electronics integrator (name withheld; the company in this demo is fictional) needed 250 housings for a sensor pod in under three weeks. Two bearing bores on opposite faces had to be coaxial within 0.0005 in, and the walls were 0.060 in thick, thin enough to spring when clamped.

## What we did

- Programmed the part for one 5-axis setup, so both bores came from the same datum instead of a flip.
- Left 0.020 in of stock, stress-relieved the roughed blanks overnight, then finished. Thin walls moved during roughing, not after finishing.
- Designed a vacuum fixture so nothing clamped on the walls.
- Checked bore position on the CMM on the first five, then every tenth part.

## Result

Shipped in 12 working days from PO. Measured bore coaxiality ranged from 0.0001 to 0.0004 in. The customer's receiving inspection accepted all 250 and reordered 600 for the next build.
