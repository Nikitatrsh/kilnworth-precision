Turning is the fastest way to make a round part. Our multi-axis lathes add milling spindles and a sub-spindle, so a shaft with a keyway, a cross hole and a turned back face comes off finished, with no second operation on a mill.

## What fits

Up to 12 in diameter and 24 in long between centers. Bar work up to 2.625 in diameter runs unattended through the night on bar feeders.

## What we hold

- ±0.005 in standard.
- ±0.0005 in on diameters and runout-critical features, checked in process with air gauging on repeat jobs.
- Surface finish down to 16 µin Ra on turned diameters without grinding.

## Typical work

Pump shafts, bearing housings, threaded adapters, hydraulic fittings, pneumatic cylinder end caps.
