Most of our work is milled. A 5-axis machine reaches five sides of a block without re-clamping it, which matters for two reasons: position tolerances between features on different faces hold, because they come from the same setup, and lead time drops, because one setup replaces three.

## What fits

Parts up to 30 × 24 × 20 in on the largest machine. Most 5-axis work is smaller than a shoebox: housings, manifolds, brackets, surgical instrument bodies, optical mounts.

## What we hold

- ±0.005 in on any dimension without a callout, our standard.
- ±0.0005 in on bores, bearing fits and datum features when the drawing asks for it.
- ±0.0002 in on selected features by agreement, with the measurement method agreed before we quote.

## When 3-axis is the better buy

If every feature can be reached from the top and one flip, a 3-axis machine is cheaper per hour. We quote the cheaper route and tell you why.
