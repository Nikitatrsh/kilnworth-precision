Wire EDM cuts conductive metal with electrical sparks from a moving brass wire. There is no cutting force, so the part does not deflect, and hardness does not matter: a die section at 60 HRC cuts as cleanly as soft steel.

## What it is good for

- Internal corners down to 0.006 in radius, where an end mill would leave 0.030 in or more.
- Punch and die sections, extrusion dies, gear profiles, splines.
- Thin slots and webs in titanium and Inconel that would chatter on a mill.

## What we hold

- ±0.0005 in standard, because the process is that consistent.
- ±0.0002 in by agreement, with skim passes to 8 µin Ra.

## Limits

Only conductive materials, and only through-features (the wire has to pass through). It is slower than milling, so we use it for the features that need it, not the whole part.
