A part is finished when it is measured, deburred, marked and packed the way the drawing says. Here the tolerance columns mean measurement uncertainty on the CMM, not machining tolerance.

## Inspection

- First article inspection to AS9102 format, with ballooned drawing, on every new part number or revision.
- CMM reports for critical features on each lot; sampling plans agreed with you.
- Material certifications and certificates of conformance with every shipment.

## In-house secondary operations

Deburring (hand and thermal-free tumbling), laser marking of part and lot numbers, ultrasonic cleaning, light assembly: press-fit pins, helicoil and threaded inserts, O-rings.

## Through approved partners

Anodizing (Type II and III), passivation, heat treatment, plating and black oxide are done by outside suppliers we audit yearly. We manage the routing and the certs; you get one PO and one delivery.
